# Idea

> Write this yourself, in plain words, before running `/define`. Five to fifteen
> sentences is plenty. Don't worry about being complete — `/define` will interview
> you about the gaps and turn this into `FSD.md`.

**What should it do?**

**Who uses it, and where (desk, wall, outdoors, battery)?**

**What does the screen show? What does touching it do?**

**Does it talk to anything (Wi-Fi, MQTT / Home Assistant, BLE, a sensor, another ESP32)?**

**What would make you say "this works"?**

**Anything you definitely do NOT want?**
